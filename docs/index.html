About Me

I am an environmental epidemiologist with a Ph.D. in Population Health Sciences from Harvard University. My research integrates epidemiology, biostatistics, and environmental science to understand how environmental and contextual factors jointly shape population health. Using population-level data, I aim to generate real-world evidence that informs public health policy and supports healthier communities.

Research Interests

  • Environmental epidemiology
  • Climate and health, particularly extreme heat and air pollution in the interaction between outdoor and indoor environments
  • Policy-relevant and translational research
  • Causal inference methods for population health
